Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

Revive Pinnacle ("Revive Pinnacle", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit our website, request information, or use our digital marketing, web design, web development, and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Business information: information you provide about your company, project, service requirements, or marketing goals.
  • Inquiry information: details submitted through our consultation or quote forms.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and similar website usage information where such information is collected through our website technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries, consultation requests, and quote requests.
  • Communicate with you about our services and your project.
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our website and services.
  • Understand website usage and improve user experience.
  • Send service-related communications or marketing communications where permitted by applicable law and where appropriate consent has been obtained.
  • Protect our website, business, users, and systems against fraud, abuse, or security threats.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Information Submitted Through Our Forms

When you submit a consultation or quote request, we may receive information such as your first name, last name, company name, phone number, email address, and service of interest.

We use this information to evaluate and respond to your request. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information through general website inquiry forms.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand website performance, and improve the user experience.

Where third-party analytics, advertising, embedded content, or other tracking technologies are used, those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party providers for services such as website hosting, analytics, email communication, form processing, advertising, security, payment processing, or other business functions. These providers may process information on our behalf or independently according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including responding to inquiries, providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal or regulatory obligations.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to provide our services, work with service providers, comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or prevent fraud and security threats.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of certain information.

You may also contact us regarding marketing communications and request that we stop sending promotional communications where applicable.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and general audiences and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website.

11. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of those third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

12. International Visitors

If you access our website from outside the country where our business or service providers are located, your information may be transferred to and processed in another country. By using our website, you acknowledge that such transfers may occur subject to applicable legal requirements.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
